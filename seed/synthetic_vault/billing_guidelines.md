# Billing & Invoicing Guidelines

## Standard Invoice Terms
- Payment terms: Net 14 days.
- Tax format: All invoices issued in NZ must include a 15% GST breakdown.
- Preferred format: Structured Markdown table with subtotal, GST, and final balance.

## Contact Information
- Inquiries: billing-team@example.internal
- Audit Frequency: Weekly on Monday mornings.
